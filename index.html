FEIYAN FU 伏飞燕

My work focuses on dexterous manipulation and embodied AI — teaching a 16-DoF hand to grasp objects it has never seen, and keeping those policies alive across the gap between simulation and a real arm.

I am a robotics engineer at LinkerBot (灵心巧手), working on motion control and vision-language-action models for dexterous hands. My work covers the whole loop: closed-loop joint control over CAN/RS485, teleoperation and hand retargeting for VLA data collection, URDF dynamics calibration against bench measurements, and on-device inference of ACT and Diffusion Policy.

I care about the gap between a policy that works in simulation and one that survives a real hand.

Publications & Competitions

Route Models, Not Modalities: What Adaptive Computation Can and Cannot Save in Multimodal Meme Understanding

Under review · NAACL 2027

Text-first modality routing collapses once images are skipped (78.1 → 68.2 macro-F1, under the 73.1 text-only baseline). Routing models instead defers the 20% least-confident memes to SigLIP-B/16 for 51–53% of the FLOPs at −0.4 to +0.4 F1, beating the best modality router by 2.5–4.7 points at equal compute.

Does Visually Grounded Reasoning Warn of Forgetting? Two Pre-registered Tests in Vision-Language Fine-tuning

Under review · CVPR 2027

Qwen2.5-VL-3B on ScienceQA, LoRA against full vision-encoder tuning, scored once on sealed seeds. Adding the visually grounded rationale likelihood moved the seed-level Brier score by 0.0001 (95% CI −0.0006 to +0.0008) and 0.0010 (+0.0003 to +0.0017) — both below the pre-registered 0.005 effect, while LoRA on three interference tasks forgets at most 3.5 points.

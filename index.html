I work on the seam between a policy that scores well in simulation and a hand that keeps its grip on a real object.

Publications & Competitions

Route Models, Not Modalities: What Adaptive Computation Can and Cannot Save in Multimodal Meme Understanding

Under review · NAACL 2027

Text-first routing collapses once images are skipped (78.1 → 68.2 macro-F1); routing models instead defers the 20% least-confident memes to SigLIP-B/16 for 51–53% of the FLOPs at −0.4 to +0.4 F1, beating the best modality router by 2.5–4.7 points at equal compute.

Conference

Does Visually Grounded Reasoning Warn of Forgetting? Two Pre-registered Tests in Vision-Language Fine-tuning

Under review · CVPR 2027

Qwen2.5-VL-3B on ScienceQA, LoRA against full vision-encoder tuning, scored once on sealed seeds. The visually grounded rationale likelihood moved the Brier score by 0.0001 (95% CI −0.0006 to +0.0008) and 0.0010 — both below the pre-registered 0.005 effect, while LoRA on three interference tasks forgets at most 3.5 points.
